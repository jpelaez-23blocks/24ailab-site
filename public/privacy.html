Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how 2xAI Lab ("we", "us") handles information when you visit 24ailab.com and fm.24ailab.com (together, the "Site").

Information we collect

The Site has no forms and no accounts, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address or phone number through it. If you email us, we receive only what you choose to send and use it to reply.

Technical data

Like most websites, our hosting and network providers may process standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type and request time) to deliver the Site and protect it from abuse. We do not use this data to identify you.

Analytics

We use Google Tag Manager (container GTM-WS2SNTLN) and Google Analytics 4 on 24ailab.com and fm.24ailab.com to understand how the Site is used, in aggregate. If you accept cookies, Google Analytics may collect the pages you view, your approximate location (derived from your IP address), your device and browser type, and the page that referred you.

On fm.24ailab.com, it may also record listening events: when a station is played, stopped or changed, how long you listen, the country you select on the globe, and the player skin you use. We do not send your name, email address or other directly identifying information to Google Analytics.

Cookies and consent

We use Google Consent Mode v2. Analytics and advertising storage are off by default, and nothing is sent to Google Analytics until you choose Accept in the cookie banner. If you choose Decline, analytics stays off. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage under the key cookie-consent, on your device, and is not sent to us.

Changing or withdrawing consent

You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer and choosing Decline. If the banner cannot be reopened, clearing this Site's data in your browser (cookies and local storage) removes your choice and brings the banner back. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google as service provider

Google processes analytics data on our behalf. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and about how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Data retention

Analytics data is kept according to our Google Analytics data retention settings and is then deleted by Google.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share data only with service providers needed to run the Site, or when required by law.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal information we hold about you. Contact us to make a request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
