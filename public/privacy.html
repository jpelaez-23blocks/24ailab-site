Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This policy explains how 2xAI Lab ("we", "us") handles information when you visit 24ailab.com (the "Site").

Information we collect

The Site is informational only. It has no forms and no accounts, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address or phone number through it. If you email us, we receive only what you choose to send and use it to reply.

Technical data

Like most websites, our hosting and network providers may process standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type and request time) to deliver the Site and protect it from abuse. We do not use this data to identify you.

Analytics and cookies

The Site does not currently set analytics cookies. We may add Google Analytics in the future to understand anonymous, aggregate usage (for example, how many people visit and which pages are viewed). Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. It would load only if you accept cookies.

Cookie consent

When you first visit, a banner at the bottom of the page asks whether you accept cookies. If you choose Accept, analytics may load; if you choose Decline, it will not. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage on your device, not sent to us. You can change it at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer.

How to opt out

You can decline in the cookie banner or via Cookie settings, block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share data only with service providers needed to run the Site, or when required by law.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal information we hold about you. Contact us to make a request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted here with a new effective date.
